Integrations break way too often

High-stakes integrations are painful to build, and can feel impossible to maintain.

The typical path for challenging integrations is to exceed deadlines and go over budget. When the integration is finally built, the data sent to the other side is incorrect.

Mistakes in these integrations cost real money: chargebacks, payment delays, supplier disputes, compliance fines, broken contract terms, claim denials, and unbilled revenue.

There is only one way out

Businesses try to fix mistakes in high stakes integrations by hiring replacement developers, adding project managers, building integrations in raw code, and, now, throwing large language models blindly at the chaos.

Merlion.bot is the integration platform that enforces correctness programmatically. It works like this:

  1. Merlion.bot works with your team to build out example-driven scenario tests.
  2. Your workflow is tested against every scenario before every deployment.
  3. In production, you update integrations by updating scenarios, not dragging fields around in a canvas and hoping it won't break.
